USDA Foreign Agricultural Service · Production, Supply & Distribution

Brazil — Coffee, Green

The PSD country table, market year July–June, MY 2010/11 onward. Each GAIN release publishes two columns per year — USDA Official, the PSD database, and New Post, the attaché's revision — so the sheet keeps both and shows one at a time. Every column balances on its own: beginning stocks + production + imports = exports + consumption + ending stocks.

Unit 1,000 × 60 kg bags, green bean equivalent —

Reading the sheet

Each yeartakes the most recent release that published the selected column; hover any filled cell for the release it came from.
Italic cellthe selected column was not published for that year, so the other column of the same release is shown instead.
Balance Checkthe one derived column: supply minus distribution, computed from the full PSD detail (imports and the export / consumption splits stay in the data). Zero on every loaded year; a non-zero value in red would mean the row mixes vintages.
Em dashno USDA release loaded yet for that year.
Area & treesfrom the release where it publishes them, otherwise the USDA series carried in the base sheet; yield is recomputed as production ÷ area harvested.
