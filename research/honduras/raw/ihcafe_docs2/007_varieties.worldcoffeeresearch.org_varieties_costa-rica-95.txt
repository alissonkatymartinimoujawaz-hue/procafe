Coffee leaf rust
Is the plant sus­cep­ti­ble to leaf rust?

Coffee Berry Disease
Is the plant sus­cep­ti­ble to CBD?
CBD is a cof­fee dis­ease that affects the fruit. It is caused by the fun­gus, Col­letotrichum kahawe. Cur­rent­ly, CBD is not present in Cen­tral Amer­i­ca, but it is a con­cern that the dis­ease will spread. Note: Plant dis­eases are con­stant­ly evolv­ing. What spac­ing should you use for plant­i­ng this vari­ety? Note: In Cen­tral Amer­i­ca, trees are typ­i­cal­ly pruned to have one main stem. In Africa, it is typ­i­cal to prune trees for mul­ti­ple (2−3) stems per tree. Additional agronomic information
Close
Additional agronomic information
Recently, Costa Rica 95 has been confirmed through scientific evaluation to be susceptible to coffee leaf rust in Costa Rica and may also be susceptible in other areas of Central America. Susceptible to Ojo de Gallo. Recommended for acidic soils and soils rich in aluminum. Recommended for warmest zones.
