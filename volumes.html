Certificates of origin are issued when the coffee is sold for export and sits in a warehouse ready to go; customs clearance and shipment follow days or weeks later. Certified − shipped (month-to-date) is therefore a proxy of the export coffee waiting in the port and inland warehouses. Vitória (ES) and REDEX/EADI (MG) are the two units located in the states tracked here. "Previous month" is the figure Cecafé shows next to the cumulative.
