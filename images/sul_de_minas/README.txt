Drop your report screenshots here — they show up in the website exactly as-is.

NAMING (very important — the site loads images by this exact name):

    <city>_<year>.png

City ids:
    varginha        -> Varginha
    carmo_de_minas  -> Carmo de Minas
    boa_esperanca   -> Boa Esperança
    guape           -> Guapé

Examples:
    varginha_2026.png        (Varginha, 2026 report graphs)
    varginha_2025.png
    carmo_de_minas_2026.png
    guape_2024.png

Each file = ONE image containing that city/year's graphs (screenshot the graphs
section of your report). Open the site and pick the year in the city tab.

Accept .png or .jpg — if you use .jpg, tell me and I'll switch the extension,
or just save as .png.

After adding/replacing images, refresh the page with Ctrl+F5 (hard refresh) so the
browser doesn't show a cached version.
