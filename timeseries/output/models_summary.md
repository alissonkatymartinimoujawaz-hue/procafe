# ARMA models of the coffee balance sheet

Order selected by **AICC** over p, q ≤ 3 (p + q ≤ 4); d = 1 when the ADF test does not reject a unit root at 5%; forecast horizon 5 years.
Equation notation follows the Wikipedia page: X_t is the series, ε_t white noise, φ the AR and θ the MA coefficients; in a linear trend b·t, t = 1 for the first observed year of the series.
Backtest MAPE = mean absolute percentage error over the last hidden years (model re-estimated without them); 'naive' = forecasting last observed value.
